⚠️ EDUCATIONAL SIMULATION
This is a fictional Digital Arrest scam demonstration.
📞 Incoming Video Call

Unknown Caller
Caller:

Your identity information has been associated with a serious criminal investigation.

Caller:

You must cooperate immediately. Disconnecting this call may create additional legal problems.

Caller:

Stay on the video call while your case is supposedly being verified.

Caller:

A payment is now supposedly required to "clear" the investigation.

🚨 DIGITAL ARREST SCAM DETECTED

What techniques were used?

😨 Fear: The victim is told that they are involved in a serious crime.
👮 Authority impersonation: The scammer pretends to represent an official authority.
⏱️ Urgency: The victim is pressured to act immediately.
🔒 Isolation: The victim may be instructed not to disconnect or contact others.
💰 Financial demand: The scam eventually attempts to obtain money from the victim.

🛡️ Reality Check

A legitimate investigation should not require you to transfer money to an unknown person simply because they claim you are under "digital arrest".

Verify claims independently using official contact channels rather than relying on information provided by the caller.
